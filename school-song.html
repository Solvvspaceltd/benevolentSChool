Our School Song

Our school song was written in May 1991, when six students decided their school deserved an anthem of its own. It is sung at assemblies and school events, and its refrain carries our motto, God and My Right.

The Lyrics

Verse 1

In the north of Sierra Leone
beyond thy field so green
A witness stands for all to see
where peace and truth prevail

Verse 2

Good people the school has made
in every sphere of life
In sports, in learning, discipline,
Humility and all

Verse 3

Those whose thoughts to build a school
Women with foresight and truth
Now sheds its prowess overall
Let's sing hurray! Hurray!

Verse 4

We now show the light to all
so help us to uphold
Proprietresses, principal and staff
Our goal we will achieve

Refrain, sung after every verse

With God and My Right, we strive
to maintain thy name others have set
Benevolent of Makeni
may Allah be thy praise

How the Song Began

In 1991 Benevolent had no official song, while its main rival proudly sang one at every inter-school competition. The Head Boy, B.I.B. Kargbo, gathered five fellow Lower Sixth Form students: Thomas Kanu, Minkailu Timbo, Emmanuel Topido Conteh, Gibrilla Bangura and Alusine Koroma, popularly known as PRO. They had one mission: to give their school a song, through every legitimate means available.

They spent their free periods writing lyrics, but without musical training they made little progress. Then a young teacher, Mr Moi Foray, overheard them from outside the classroom window. He suggested they ask Mr Wyse, Head of Music at the then Makeni Teachers College, an institution now associated with the Ernest Bai Koroma University of Science and Technology.

Mr Wyse asked about the school's history, structure and character, and a week later the song was ready. The six students put together a small purse to thank him. One evening in May 1991 he sat at the piano in the music classroom and rehearsed them until they knew every verse, and they recorded the song on a new cassette tape.

On the day of the unveiling, the Principal, Mr E.S.A. Kargbo, was away from morning assembly, so the pioneers taught the song to the rest of the Sixth Form while they waited. At about 10 a.m., with the Principal back at school, they gathered below his office and began to sing. He came out to find them, called a second assembly on the spot, and the song was introduced to the whole school.

We were six young students with one shared dream. We did not merely wish for a school song; we took the initiative to make it happen.

Dr B.I.B. Kargbo, Head Boy in 1991

The Pioneers

  • B.I.B. Kargbo, Head Boy
  • Thomas Kanu
  • Minkailu Timbo
  • Emmanuel Topido Conteh
  • Gibrilla Bangura
  • Alusine Koroma (PRO)

With thanks to Mr Moi Foray, whose advice pointed the way; Mr Wyse, who composed the music; and Mr E.S.A. Kargbo, then Principal, who brought the song to the whole school.
