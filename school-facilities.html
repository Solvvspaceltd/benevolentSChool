School Facilities

Our modern facilities support comprehensive academic and extracurricular programs.

Library & Resource Center

Comprehensive collection of textbooks, journals, digital resources, and quiet study areas.

Science Laboratories

Equipped biology, chemistry, and physics labs for practical experiments and research.

Computer Labs

Multiple computer labs with modern software and internet connectivity for all students.

Lecture Halls

Modern classrooms with interactive displays, projectors, and comfortable seating.

Prayer Facilities

Dedicated prayer halls for daily prayers and Islamic religious observances.

Sports Complex

Football pitch, basketball courts, volleyball courts, and athletics track.

Dining Facilities

Modern cafeteria serving nutritious meals meeting dietary requirements.

Health Center

On-site clinic with qualified medical staff and emergency response capabilities.

About BISS

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School is committed to providing quality education rooted in Islamic values, preparing students for academic excellence and responsible citizenship.
