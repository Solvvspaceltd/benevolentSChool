School Library

The Benevolent Islamic Secondary School Library is a vibrant learning hub designed to support student learning, research, and personal development. Our library provides a comprehensive collection of resources and a welcoming environment for all students.

Library Resources

Book Collection

  • Academic textbooks covering all subject areas
  • Reference materials and encyclopedias
  • Literature and fiction for recreational reading
  • Islamic studies and religious texts
  • Biography and history collections

Digital Resources

  • Access to online databases and journals
  • E-books and digital publications
  • Educational videos and multimedia materials
  • Internet access for research

Library Services

  • Book borrowing and returns (standard loan period: 2 weeks)
  • Reference assistance and research support
  • Library orientation for new students
  • Information literacy instruction
  • Quiet study areas and group work spaces
  • Computer terminals for research and study

Operating Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM (limited hours)
Closed: Sundays and public holidays

Library Rules & Regulations

  • Maintain silence and respect other users
  • Return borrowed materials on time
  • Do not write on or damage library materials
  • Respect the library classification system
  • Use computers responsibly and appropriately

Librarian & Support Staff

Our experienced librarians are available to assist with research queries, book recommendations, and information literacy skills development. They are dedicated to helping students make the most of library resources.

About BISS

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School is committed to providing quality education rooted in Islamic values, preparing students for academic excellence and responsible citizenship.
