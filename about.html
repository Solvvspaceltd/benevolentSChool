About Benevolent Islamic Secondary School

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School, Makeni, was founded in 1979 by the Sierra Leone Muslim Women's Benevolent Organisation (SLMWBO) to give young people, whatever their background, access to quality education rooted in Islamic values.

Our Mission

To serve the poor and needy by providing accessible, quality and holistic education, promoting Islamic values and moral development, and supporting the welfare of disadvantaged individuals and communities through healthcare, clothing and other humanitarian services, thereby empowering individuals and contributing to the social, moral and economic development of Sierra Leone.

Our Vision

To become a leading Islamic humanitarian and educational organisation committed to building an enlightened, morally upright, educated and self-reliant society in which every individual, regardless of economic or social background, has access to quality education, Islamic knowledge, healthcare and basic necessities for a dignified life.

Our Core Values

Service to Humanity

Demonstrating compassion and commitment to serving the poor and needy.

Islamic Faith and Values

Promoting Islamic teachings, moral discipline, honesty and righteousness.

Quality Education

Providing inclusive and quality education as a foundation for individual and national development.

Compassion and Charity

Supporting vulnerable children, women, families and disadvantaged communities.

Equality and Inclusiveness

Ensuring that opportunities are accessible to people regardless of their economic and social circumstances.

Integrity and Accountability

Upholding honesty, transparency and responsible management of resources.

National Development

Contributing to Sierra Leone's progress through education, healthcare, moral upbringing and humanitarian assistance.

School Facilities

  • State-of-the-art classrooms with modern learning resources
  • Well-equipped computer laboratory with internet connectivity
  • Comprehensive school library with extensive collection of resources
  • Sports facilities including football pitch and basketball court
  • Assembly hall for school events and gatherings
  • Mosque for Islamic prayers and spiritual development

Our Community

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School is more than just an institution of learning. It is a vibrant community of students, staff, parents, and stakeholders working together to achieve excellence. We believe in the power of collaboration and partnership in fostering student success.

About BISS

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School is committed to providing quality education rooted in Islamic values, preparing students for academic excellence and responsible citizenship.
