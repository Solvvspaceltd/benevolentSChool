Senior Level Guide

Our Senior Level Programme (Forms 3-4) is designed to challenge and inspire students as they progress towards their final examinations. During these crucial years, students engage in rigorous academic study while developing the critical thinking, research, and problem-solving skills essential for success in higher education and the professional world.

Programme Overview

The Senior Level spans two academic years and prepares students for the West African Senior Secondary Certificate Examination (WASSCE) and other standardized assessments. Our curriculum emphasizes not only subject mastery but also the development of independent learning skills, intellectual curiosity, and academic integrity.

Key Features

  • Specialized Subject Streams: Students select subjects aligned with their academic strengths and future aspirations
  • Advanced Teaching Methods: Emphasizing analysis, synthesis, and evaluation of complex concepts
  • Research and Projects: Culminating projects and research activities in most subjects
  • Examination Preparation: Dedicated preparation sessions and mock examinations
  • University Counselling: Guidance on university selection, applications, and career planning
  • Leadership Opportunities: Senior students serve as mentors and leaders within the school community

Subject Offerings

At the Senior Level, students pursue a balanced curriculum that typically includes:

  • Languages: English Language, English Literature, Arabic, French, Krio
  • Mathematics: Core Mathematics, Further Mathematics
  • Sciences: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Integrated Science
  • Social Sciences: History, Geography, Government, Economics
  • Humanities: Islamic Studies, Literature, Philosophy
  • Technical Subjects: Information Technology, Technical Drawing

Academic Support and Enrichment

We provide comprehensive support to ensure every student reaches their potential:

  • Personalized academic mentoring and tutoring services
  • Study skills workshops and time management seminars
  • Access to digital learning resources and online platforms
  • Subject-specific revision clinics before major examinations
  • Career development workshops and professional exposure

Pastoral Care and Wellbeing

The Senior Level is an intense period of study, and we ensure students receive comprehensive pastoral support. Form teachers, counsellors, and senior leadership work closely with students to manage stress, maintain mental health, and ensure a balanced approach to academic and personal development.

Progression to Sixth Form and Beyond

Successful completion of the Senior Level programme opens pathways to our Sixth Form programme, universities within Sierra Leone, and international tertiary institutions. Students with excellent results can pursue higher education in competitive programmes across the region and globally.

Get Involved

The Senior Level is not just about academics. Students continue to participate in sports, house competitions, music programmes, and a variety of clubs and societies, ensuring a well-rounded and fulfilling educational experience.

Questions about our Senior Level Programme?

Contact our Academic Affairs Office for more information about curriculum, subject combinations, and academic support services.
